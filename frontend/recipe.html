About this Dish

Paneer Butter Masala is a rich North Indian curry made with paneer, tomato, butter, cream, and aromatic spices. It is believed to have originated around the Delhi and Punjab region, inspired by popular restaurant-style tomato-butter gravies. Today, it is widely eaten across India and in Indian restaurants around the world as a popular vegetarian dish.

INGREDIENTS

  • 200g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 cup cream or milk
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

NUTRITIONAL VALUE

Approximate values per serving

  • Calories: 350–450 kcal
  • Protein: 14–18g
  • Carbohydrates: 15–20g
  • Fat: 25–32g
  • Fiber: 3–5g
  • Calcium: High, mainly from paneer

RECIPE STEPS

  1. Heat butter and oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add chopped onion and cook until soft and light golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for one minute.
  4. Add tomato puree, turmeric, red chili powder, and salt.
  5. Cook until the tomato mixture thickens and oil starts separating.
  6. Add cream or milk and mix well to create a smooth gravy.
  7. Add paneer cubes and simmer for 4–5 minutes.
  8. Add garam masala and mix gently.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with naan or rice.
